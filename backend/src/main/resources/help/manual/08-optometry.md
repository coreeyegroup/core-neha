# Optometry
> The work-up queue: vision, refraction and eye pressure before the doctor sees the patient.
<!-- audience: eye.optometry.record -->

Who uses it: optometrists. Opens from **Clinic → Optometry**.

## The queue

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| My queue | Patients waiting for optometry, by token, with waiting time |
| With the doctor | Patients you have sent on |
| All today | Every visit today |

Cards at the top: waiting for optometry (and the longest wait), sent to the doctor, visits today, completed today.

## Recording the work-up

Click a patient to open the [visit record](/help/manual/the-visit-record). The **Optometry** section is a table with a column for each eye: **Right eye (RE)** and **Left eye (LE)**.

| Row | How to enter |
|---|---|
| Unaided distance vision | Choose or type Snellen: `6/6`, `6/9`, `6/60`, `CF` (counting fingers), `HM` (hand movements), `PL` (perception of light), `NPL` |
| With present glasses | Same, with the patient's current spectacles |
| Near vision | `N6`, `N8`, `N12`… |
| Sphere, Cylinder (D) | Dioptres with sign, for example `+1.00`, `-0.75` |
| Axis (°) | 0 to 180, with a cylinder |
| Near add (D) | 0 to 4 |
| Best corrected vision | Snellen with the best refraction |
| Eye pressure, IOP (mmHg) | 0 to 80, and the **IOP method** (non-contact, applanation, Tono-Pen, iCare) |

Also **Colour vision** and **Notes**.

- **Save** keeps your work; you can come back to it.
- **Complete and send to doctor** moves the patient to the doctor's queue. The system asks for at least the vision of the eyes first.

Until the doctor completes the consultation you (and the doctor) can still correct the optometry.

> **Note** Front office does not see these findings; only clinical staff do.
