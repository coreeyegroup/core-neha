# Consultation
> The doctor's queue, examination, diagnosis, treatment, and advising surgery.
<!-- audience: eye.consultation.record -->

Who uses it: consultant and associate ophthalmologists. Opens from **Clinic → Consultation**.

## The queue

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Ready for consultation | Optometry done, waiting for a doctor |
| In optometry | Coming next |
| All today | Every visit today |

**Only my patients (and unassigned)** filters to patients booked with you or with no doctor chosen.

Cards: ready for you, in optometry, sent to counselling, open surgery advice, surgeries today, completed visits.

## Recording the consultation

Open the patient. The visit record shows the optometry results above the **Consultation** section:

1. **History and presenting complaint**.
2. **Anterior segment** and **Fundus / posterior segment**, for each eye.
3. **Diagnosis**, the **ICD-10** code (for example `H25.1` immature senile cataract, `H40.1` primary open-angle glaucoma, `H52.1` myopia) and the **Eye**.
4. **Medicines**, one per line: drug, dose, eye, how often, how long.
5. **Advice** and **Follow-up in (days)**.
6. Tick **Spectacles prescribed** when glasses are given from the refraction.

**Save** keeps a draft. **Complete and send to checkout** finishes the consultation. A diagnosis is required to complete.

You can examine a patient before optometry is complete; the screen tells you so.

## Advising surgery

Tick **Surgery advised**, then choose the **Procedure** (or type one), the **Operative eye** and the **Urgency** (*Routine*, *Within 2 weeks*, *Urgent*). The button becomes **Complete and send to counselling**. On completion:

- The patient moves to **Surgery counselling**.
- A **surgery case** is created with status *Advised*.

After completion the consultation cannot be changed in this version of the system.
