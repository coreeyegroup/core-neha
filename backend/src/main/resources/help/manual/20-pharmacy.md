# Pharmacy
> Prescriptions from doctors, dispensing against a prescription or over the counter, batches and expiry, bills, payments and returns.
<!-- audience: pharmacy.read pharmacy.dispense pharmacy.return pharmacy.prescribe -->

Who uses it: pharmacists and pharmacy assistants at the counter, and doctors who prescribe. Opens from **Pharmacy and optical → Pharmacy**.

## Writing a prescription (doctors)

1. Open the patient's visit (from the consultation desk or the patient record).
2. In **Medicines**, choose **Prescribe**.
3. For each medicine: choose it from the list, then dose, how often, number of days, which eye, and quantity.
4. **Send to pharmacy**. The prescription appears in the pharmacy queue straight away with a number such as `VEC/RX/2026-27/000012`.

A prescription can be cancelled by a prescriber until something has been dispensed on it.

## Dispensing against a prescription

1. **Prescriptions waiting** lists every open prescription at the centre. Click one.
2. The dialog shows each medicine as prescribed and how much is still to be given. Change a quantity if the patient takes less.
3. **Dispense and bill**. The system:
   - takes stock from the pharmacy store, **earliest expiry first**, and never an expired batch;
   - records the batch numbers on the sale, so any batch can be traced to the patients who received it;
   - raises the bill (MRP includes GST) and offers to collect payment at once.

A prescription older than 30 days must be renewed by the doctor.

## Over-the-counter sales

**Over-the-counter sale** sells medicines without a prescription, for example lubricant eye drops. Choose the patient (register them first if new), add medicines and quantities, and dispense.

> **Schedule H, H1 and X medicines are never sold without the doctor's prescription in the system.** The counter refuses them, whatever the role of the person dispensing.

## Payments

Pharmacy bills are ordinary bills. Collect at the counter (**Collect** on the sale) or at reception. They appear in Accounts collections and the day close. A pharmacy charge cannot be removed from a bill at the billing counter; take the medicine back with a return instead.

## Returns

**Sales → Return** takes unopened medicine back into the same batch within 30 days. Expired medicine cannot go back into stock. The returned amount is shown on the sale; refund it through accounts.

## Stock

The pharmacy's stock, batches, expiry and reorder levels are in **Inventory and stores** (select the Pharmacy store). Medicines expiring within 90 days are listed under **Expiry**.
