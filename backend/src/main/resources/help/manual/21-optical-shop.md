# Optical shop
> Spectacle orders from the optometry prescription, frames and lenses from stock or the lab, bills, delivery and cancellation.
<!-- audience: optical.read optical.order optical.deliver -->

Who uses it: optical sales executives and the optical store in-charge. Opens from **Pharmacy and optical → Optical shop**.

## Taking a spectacle order

1. **New spectacle order**, then find the patient.
2. **Prescription**: choose the refraction from one of the patient's visits. It is copied from the optometry record when you place the order and **cannot be changed afterwards**, so the glasses always match what the optometrist recorded. For a prescription from outside, choose *Outside prescription* and type it in.
3. Measure and enter the pupillary distance for each eye; for progressive lenses also the fitting height (required).
4. **Frame**: from our stock (choose it; one is taken from stock) or the patient's own frame.
5. **Lenses**: choose the lens from the item master, then
   - **In our optical lab**: a job goes to the optical lab with a frozen specification; or
   - **From stock, fitted in the shop**: choose the stock lens pair; it is taken from stock.
6. Set the promised date and **Place order and bill**. The bill for frame and lenses is raised at once; take an advance or the full amount with **Collect**.

## Following an order

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| In the lab | The lab is making the lenses; the lab's current stage is shown under the status |
| Fitting in shop | Stock lenses are being fitted in the shop; **Mark ready** when done |
| Ready for patient | Back from the lab (dispatched) or fitted in the shop |
| Delivered | Handed to the patient |

Overdue orders (past the promised date) are counted at the top of the page.

## Delivery

**Deliver** is available only when the bill is fully paid. Collect the balance first.

## Cancelling

**Cancel** asks for a reason. The frame and any stock lenses go back into stock, the lab job is stopped, and the unpaid bill is cancelled. If something was already paid, refund it through accounts first.
