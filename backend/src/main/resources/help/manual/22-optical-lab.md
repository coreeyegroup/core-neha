# Optical lab (manufacturing)
> Lens jobs from the shops: surfacing, coating, edging, fitting, quality check by a second person, and dispatch.
<!-- audience: opticallab.read opticallab.process opticallab.qc -->

Who uses it: surfacing technicians, edging and fitting technicians, quality inspectors and the lab supervisor. Opens from **Pharmacy and optical → Optical lab**.

## The job board

Each spectacle order with lab lenses becomes a job card (`VEC/LAB/2026-27/000031`). The board has a column for each stage:

Received → Surfacing → Coating → Edging → Fitting → Quality check → Ready → Dispatched

Click a card to open it. The **specification** (both eyes' powers, pupillary distance, fitting height, lens, coating, tint and frame) was frozen when the shop placed the order; nobody can change it in the lab.

## Moving a job

- **Received → Surfacing**: choose the lens blank. A pair of blanks is taken from the lab store automatically.
- **Surfacing → Coating** (or straight to **Edging** for uncoated lenses).
- **Edging → Fitting → Fitting done, send to QC**: the person who sends it to QC is recorded as the fitter.

Every move is recorded with who and when (the history at the bottom of the card).

## Quality check

The inspector measures the finished spectacles on the lensmeter and enters the sphere, cylinder and axis for each eye.

- **Pass** is refused if any power is off by more than 0.13 D (0.25 D above 6 D), or the axis by more than 3° (7° for cylinders under 0.50 D).
- **The fitter cannot check their own work**; another person must.
- **Fail, remake** sends the job back to *Received*; the remake count goes up and new blanks are used.

## Dispatch

**Dispatch to shop** (quality inspectors and the supervisor) marks the order *Ready for patient* in the optical shop.

## Lens blanks

Blank stock, batches and reorder levels are in **Inventory and stores** (select the Optical lab store). Receive blanks from suppliers there.
