# Surgery counselling
> Explaining surgery, packages and cost, recording the patient's decision, and booking the OT date.

Who uses it: surgery counsellors and patient care coordinators. Opens from **Clinic → Surgery counselling**.

## The lists

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| To counsel | Surgery advised by a doctor, not yet counselled |
| To schedule | Accepted by the patient, no date yet (with the preferred date) |
| Booked | Scheduled or ready surgeries from today on |
| All recent | The last 200 surgery cases |

Click a row to open the surgery.

## Counselling

On the surgery page, the **Counselling** section:

1. **Surgery package**: from the centre's price list (packages such as phaco with monofocal IOL, premium toric IOL, SICS, pterygium). The **Estimated cost** fills in from the price; change it if the package differs.
2. **Payment**: *Self pay*, *Insurance / TPA* (then enter the **Insurer / TPA**), *Corporate*, *Government scheme*.
3. **Preferred date** and **Counselling notes** (what was explained: procedure, risks, recovery, costs, who was present).
4. The patient's decision: **Accepts surgery**, **Will decide later**, or **Declines**.
5. **Save decision**.

Accepting needs a package. After any decision, the patient's visit moves to **Checkout** so reception can bill the day's charges and send them home. A patient who will decide later stays in *To counsel*.

## Booking the OT slot

For an accepted surgery, the **Schedule** section: **Date**, **Time**, **Theatre** (for example OT 1) and **Surgeon** (staff of the operation theatre at this centre). Choose **Book OT slot**. The system refuses a slot where the surgeon already has a surgery. Use **Reschedule** to change it.

**Cancel surgery…** (with a reason) is available until the surgery is completed.
