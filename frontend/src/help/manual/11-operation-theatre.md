# Operation theatre
> The OT list, the pre-operative checklist, the operative note, and the automatic day-1 review.

Who uses it: ophthalmic surgeons and OT nurses. Opens from **Clinic → Operation theatre**.

## The OT list

Choose the **OT date** (today by default). The list shows time, patient, procedure and eye, diagnosis, surgeon, package, checklist and status. **Booked ahead** lists later surgeries.

Cards: on the list, checklist pending, ready for theatre, completed, booked ahead.

Status: *Scheduled* → *Ready for theatre* → *Completed*.

## Pre-operative checklist (OT nurse)

Open the surgery. In **Pre-operative checklist** tick:

- **Informed consent signed**
- **Operative eye marked** (the page shows which eye)
- **Medically fit** (BP, sugar, ECG as needed)
- **Biometry done, IOL power chosen**
- **Pupil dilated**

and enter **IOL power**, **Blood pressure**, **Blood sugar** and **Notes**. **Save** keeps progress; **Mark ready for theatre** needs consent, eye marking and fitness all ticked. The database refuses a "ready" surgery without them.

## Operative note (surgeon)

When the surgery is *Ready for theatre*, the **Operation** section opens for the surgeon:

- **Anaesthesia**: topical, peribulbar, retrobulbar, sub-Tenon or general.
- **IOL implanted**: type, power, lot.
- **Operative notes**, **Complications** ("None" if none), **Post-operative instructions**.

**Save** keeps a draft. **Complete surgery** needs anaesthesia and notes. On completion the system:

- marks the surgery *Completed* with the surgeon and time;
- books a **post-operative review for the next day at 10:00** with the operating surgeon (reception sees it in tomorrow's appointments).
