Your visit

  1. Call or message

    Call or WhatsApp to confirm the doctor's timings, or tell reception you are on your way. Walk-ins are welcome.

  2. Registration and tests

    Register at the front desk; an optometrist checks your vision, glasses power and eye pressure.

  3. Consultation

    A senior specialist examines your eyes with the slit lamp, and dilates the pupils when needed.

  4. Your plan

    The doctor explains the diagnosis and treatment, your spectacle prescription, or surgery and cashless Mediclaim.

What to bring

  • Your current glasses, and contact lens details if you wear them.
  • Earlier eye reports and prescriptions, and your patient number if you have visited before.
  • A list of the medicines you take, including eye drops, and any allergies.
  • Someone to accompany you if your pupils may be dilated: vision stays blurred for a few hours.

Surgery day

  • Follow the fasting instructions you are given; take your usual medicines unless the doctor advised otherwise.
  • Come with an adult who can take you home, without make-up around the eyes.
  • Before surgery the team confirms your consent, marks the eye, checks blood pressure and sugar, and dilates the pupil.
  • After surgery: use the drops as prescribed, wear the shield at night, do not rub the eye, and come for your review.

Cashless Mediclaim

Tell reception or the counsellor at the start that you are insured. Our Mediclaim desk handles the documents and the pre-authorisation with your insurer or TPA.

Bring for cashless processing

  • Original health insurance card or e-card
  • Government photo ID (Aadhaar or PAN card)
  • Past consultation papers and test reports
  • Policy document with the policy number

Questions

Anything else? Call or message the clinic.

Where is the clinic?

At #2, PV Plaza, 5th Main Road, MC Layout, Vijayanagar, opposite Sarvajna Bus Stop near the Govindarajanagar signal. It is about five minutes from Vijayanagar Metro Station, and BMTC buses from Majestic, Market and Rajajinagar stop in front of the clinic.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome during clinic hours. A quick call or WhatsApp message helps us confirm the doctor's timings and shortens your wait.

How long does a consultation take?

Usually one to two hours, including vision and pressure tests. If the doctor dilates your pupils to examine the retina, allow more time and do not drive yourself home.

Is cataract surgery done as day-care?

Yes. After optical biometry to choose the lens, phacoemulsification is done as day-care surgery, with monofocal, toric and multifocal lenses from Zeiss and Alcon. You go home the same day and come back for a review.

Can I find out whether LASIK suits me?

Yes. Pre-LASIK tests include corneal topography, corneal thickness (pachymetry) and a dilated retina examination.

Is cashless treatment available?

Yes, with the insurers and TPAs listed on this website. Our Mediclaim desk helps with the documents and the pre-authorisation.

Do you see children?

Yes: children's consultations, screening for refractive errors, treatment for lazy eye (amblyopia) and squint evaluation.
